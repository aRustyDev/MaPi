# Archive key custody requirements

Status: **not implemented or verified**. The local object-store helper does not encrypt archives, manage keys or establish a recoverable backup. This note defines evidence needed for the Phase 1 archive; it does not select or provision a key service.

## Independence and lifecycle

- Keep the archive decryption authority recoverable independently of the source Durable Object and its registry, including after an account, operator or credential failure covered by the recovery policy. An independently controlled key-management service or separately protected key escrow can satisfy this only after its authority, recovery and failure domains have been tested. An access-controlled Worker secret in the same operational account alone does not establish independence.
- Define the key custodian, authorized writer and recovery roles, administrative and break-glass access, audit trail, storage and key retention periods, loss and compromise procedures, and who approves destruction. The required staging retention period and actual recovery authority have not been established. Never store actual plaintext archive keys in source SQLite, archive objects, logs, test fixtures, or repository files.
- Bind an authenticated archive to an immutable key identifier and key version. A recovery operator must obtain or unwrap the correct key under the independently checked recovery authority. Rotation or rewrapping must preserve decryptability of every retained archive and its authenticated binding; do not retire or delete an old key until all dependent archives have expired under the approved retention policy and recovery has been verified.
- Use synthetic test keys locally. Cloudflare documents that [local Secrets Store values are not encrypted](https://developers.cloudflare.com/secrets-store/manage-secrets/). It says saved secret values are not retrievable via dashboard or API; a bound service can use the value. Therefore independently recoverable escrow and a tested recovery path cannot be inferred merely from creating a secret. [Editing a secret replaces its value across services using it](https://developers.cloudflare.com/secrets-store/manage-secrets/how-to/), so overwriting one secret during rotation is not evidence that old archives remain recoverable.
- Access to the archive store and access to decryption authority need separately reviewed controls. Cloudflare documents that [Secrets Store Edit permission can create, edit, duplicate or delete secrets and is required to bind a secret to another resource](https://developers.cloudflare.com/secrets-store/access-control/). The effective deployment and administrator permissions must be checked rather than assuming that a separate binding provides separation of duties.

## Evidence before an archive can support cutover

On isolated synthetic staging, recover an archive and its key after independently recreating the recovery environment. Verify successful restore with the retained key version, and rejection or safe fencing for an unavailable, wrong, revoked, retired or unauthorized key. Exercise key rotation and recovery of both old and new archives, lost acknowledgements, restart and retry, and administrator or credential loss under the chosen custody policy. Confirm permissions, audit records and retention separately for archive storage and keys; record any shared failure domains and access-control exceptions.

This key evidence is necessary but not sufficient. The archive still requires a trusted source commitment, authenticated complete seal, bounded verification, staging and reconciliation, a tested retention policy, and a verified restore as described in [archive-restore-integration-requirements.md](archive-restore-integration-requirements.md). Keep the source fence and replay chunks until all applicable move, archive and PITR gates permit cleanup.
